Heuristic & Algorithmic Nature of Results

The detection scores, risk classifications, and anomaly flags produced by isthereaiinmyplaylist are automated algorithmic estimations. They are computed by evaluating multi-layered forensics vectors:

  • Catalog Velocity & Release Density: Statistical frequency of singles/EPs uploaded per week/month.
  • Canonical Registry Presence: Cross-referencing against MusicBrainz and ISRC records.
  • Acoustic & Spectral Telemetry: High-frequency spectrogram analysis, cut-off frequency heuristics, and stereo phase artifacts commonly found in latent audio diffusion models (e.g. Suno, Udio).
  • Community & Directory Verification: Verified synthetic databases including Soul Over AI, Spotify AI Blocker DB, and crowdsourced submissions.

Important: These results are provided strictly for research, educational, and informational transparency purposes. They do not constitute a definitive legal certification of synthetic generation, copyright infringement, or non-human authorship. False positives and false negatives may occur.

Artist & Rights Holder Dispute Process

We deeply respect human artists, independent songwriters, and producers. If your music or artist profile has been misclassified as synthetic or AI-generated in error (false positive), you have the full right to prompt correction or removal.

How to request a review:

  • Send an email to dispute@isthereaiinmyplaylist.tech (or tobiasek.oliver@gmail.com).
  • Include the Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube link to the track/artist.
  • Provide brief context or evidence of human production (e.g. DAW project screenshot, studio session photo, raw stems, live performance, or distributor ISRC verification).

Verified human tracks and artists are added to an explicit human whitelist and cleared across all heuristic evaluations.

Privacy Policy & Cookieless Architecture (GDPR Compliant)

We are committed to user privacy and strict adherence to European GDPR standards:

  • Cookieless by Default: We do not set tracking cookies, marketing cookies, or third-party profiling trackers for general visitors.
