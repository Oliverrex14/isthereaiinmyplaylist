How Our 4-Layer AI Detection Engine Works

🏷️

1. Keywords & Generators

Detects traces of Suno, Udio, RVC v2, Kits.ai, Boomy, Riffusion, and explicit tags like AI Cover or [AI] across titles and channels.

🗃️

2. Known AI & Ghost Artists DB

Cross-references artists against our database of known synthetic projects (e.g. The Velvet Sundown, Endel, AIVA) and community reports.

🕵️‍♂️

3. Artist History & Release Velocity

Queries Deezer & Apple iTunes catalogs to verify if an artist existed prior to 2023, checks fanbase size, and flags mass-uploaded DIY releases.

🎛️

4. Audio Preview & Duration

Checks typical AI model duration windows (e.g. 2:00 / 4:00 limits) and inspects spectral byte entropy on available audio preview streams.
