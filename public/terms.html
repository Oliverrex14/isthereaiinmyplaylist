1. Purpose of the Service

Is There AI In My Playlist is an open-source, non-commercial research and educational platform designed to empower music fans, curators, and independent artists by analyzing streaming playlist metadata for indicators of synthetic artificial intelligence generation (such as Suno, Udio, vocal clones, and automated streaming-farm catalogs).

2. Nature of Results & Algorithmic Disclaimer

Important Forensic Notice: All synthetic percentage probabilities, risk classifications, and anomaly indicators presented on this site are automated algorithmic estimations.

  • Results are derived from combining metadata heuristics (release frequency, discography cadence, singles ratios), canonical registry cross-referencing (MusicBrainz, ISRC), community submissions, and public catalog signals.
  • These results do not constitute a definitive legal certification, judicial finding of copyright infringement, or incontrovertible proof of synthetic generation.
  • While our multi-layered algorithms include strict safeguards for pre-2023 human artists and verified catalog registries, false positives and false negatives may occasionally occur.
