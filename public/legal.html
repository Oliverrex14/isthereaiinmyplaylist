3. Heuristic & Algorithmic Nature of Analysis

The AI detection percentages, risk classifications, and anomaly indicators generated by this service are computed through automated algorithmic heuristics:

  • Catalog Velocity & Cadence: Measuring release density of singles and EPs per time window.
  • Canonical Identity Records: Cross-referencing against MusicBrainz canonical artist IDs and official ISRC registries.
  • Acoustic & Spectral Telemetry: Evaluating high-frequency spectrogram characteristics, audio duration profiles, and synthetic anomalies typical of latent audio diffusion architectures.
  • Crowdsourced Registry Matching: Cross-verifying against public AI registries (Soul Over AI, Spotify AI Blocker DB).

These results are provided strictly for research, educational, and informational transparency purposes. They do not constitute a definitive legal certification, formal copyright infringement determination, or judicially binding judgment of authorship.

4. Artist Rights, Dispute & False Positive Correction Process

We are firmly committed to protecting and championing human musicians, songwriters, and independent producers. If your music or artist discography has been misidentified by an automated heuristic:

  • We provide a prompt, human-reviewed dispute process free of charge.
  • Send an email to dispute@isthereaiinmyplaylist.tech or tobiasek.oliver@gmail.com.
  • Include your artist name, Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube URL, and any brief confirmation of human production (such as a studio photo, DAW screenshot, live recording, or distributor ISRC confirmation).
  • Once verified, human artists are placed on an explicit permanent human safeguard list that prevents automated false positives across all future scans.
